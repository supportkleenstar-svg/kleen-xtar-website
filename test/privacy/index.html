Privacy policy

What we collect when you book with us, why, and how to ask us to change or delete it.

Who we are

Kleen Xtar [registered business name and address, to confirm] provides doorstep car cleaning in Bengaluru. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name and phone number, and email if you give it for invoices.
  • Where your car is: your address, area or PIN code, society name and parking spot.
  • Your car: make, model, type and registration number [to confirm].
  • Photos: photos of your car taken before and after each clean, and of any marks or issues we notice.
  • Messages: your WhatsApp chats with us, including pause requests, ratings and complaints.
  • Payments: your plan, invoices and payment status. Card and bank details are handled by our payment provider; we don’t store them.
  • Key handovers: times and who handed over your key, on interior days.

Why we use it

  • To schedule and carry out your cleans, and to send you photos and reminders.
  • To bill you and send GST invoices.
  • To handle re-cleans, complaints and damage reports.
  • To share monthly service reports with your society’s facility manager, if you’re on a society plan. These show cars cleaned and complaints, not your payment details.
  • To tell you about changes to your plan or our service. We’ll only send marketing messages if you agree, and you can stop them any time.

Who we share it with

  • Your cleaner and crew lead, who need your car details, parking spot and WhatsApp to do the job.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), which carries our messages and photos.
  • Our payment provider [name, to confirm], to process payments.
  • Your society’s security or facility manager, for gate access and key logs.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We don’t sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you’re a customer and for [period, to confirm] afterwards, or longer where tax and accounting law requires us to keep invoices. Car photos are kept for [period, to confirm].

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you,
  • correct anything that’s wrong or out of date,
  • delete your data when you stop using our service, unless we have to keep it by law,
  • stop sending you marketing messages.

Send your request on WhatsApp and we’ll reply within [30 days, to confirm]. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
