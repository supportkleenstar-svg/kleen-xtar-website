Rats in the engine bay: the warning signs and how to protect a parked car

Protection24 November 20262 min read

A car parked near a garden, drain or open plot is a warm, quiet place for a rat. Here’s what to look for and how a monthly repellent treatment helps.

Ask around any Bengaluru society and someone will have a story. The car wouldn’t start, a warning light came on, and the mechanic found chewed wires and a nest of shredded paper on top of the engine.

A parked car is a warm, sheltered spot, and modern wiring is easy to chew through. Cars that sit near gardens, drains or open plots are the most likely to get visitors.

Signs a rat has been in your car

  • Droppings on the engine cover, battery or under the bonnet
  • Bits of paper, cloth, leaves or plastic collected in a corner of the engine bay
  • Chewed rubber, insulation or wire covering
  • A new warning light on the dashboard, or a car that suddenly won’t start
  • A bad smell through the AC vents
  • Small footprints in the dust on the bonnet or windscreen

If you see chewed wires or a warning light, take the car to your mechanic before driving it far. Damaged wiring can affect sensors, lights and starting.

Simple things that help

  • Don’t leave food, wrappers or pet food in the car or near where it’s parked.
  • Drive the car regularly if you can. A car that sits for days is more inviting than one that moves every morning.
  • Keep the area around the parking spot free of rubbish and loose cardboard.
  • Open the bonnet and take a look every couple of weeks, especially after a long trip away.

What our rodent-repellent treatment does

It takes about 30 minutes and starts from ₹399:

  1. We check the engine bay for chewed wires and nests.
  2. A non-toxic repellent is sprayed on the wiring and hoses.
  3. Repellent tape goes around the wires rats chew most.
  4. We send a reminder on WhatsApp when it’s due again next month.

Repellent wears off, so it works best as a monthly habit. It’s included every month on the Daily Plus plan, or you can book it on its own from our one-time services.
