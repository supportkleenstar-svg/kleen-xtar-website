How to bring daily car cleaning to your apartment society

Societies24 November 20262 min read

A guide for association members and facility managers: what to agree on, what to ask any car cleaning vendor, and how a free demo morning works.

In most societies, car cleaning happens informally. A few cleaners come and go, nobody knows exactly who is in the basement at 5 AM, and the association hears about it only when something goes wrong.

A single, organised crew solves most of that. Here’s how to set it up, whether you work with us or not.

What to agree on first

  • A fixed morning window, so security knows when to expect the crew.
  • Where the crew signs in, every day, in the gate register.
  • The water source: an approved tap, or the vendor’s own cans. No hose pipes in the basement.
  • How keys are handled on interior days, and who logs them.
  • Who residents complain to, and how fast complaints should be closed.

Questions to ask any vendor

  • Are the cleaners police-verified, and can we see their documents?
  • Do they wear a uniform and carry photo ID?
  • Is there a named supervisor the facility manager can call?
  • What products do they use? Kerosene, diesel or washing powder should be a no.
  • How much water does each car use, and where does it come from?
  • Will they share insurance details and timings before the first wash?
  • Will the association get a regular report on attendance and complaints?

How we start in a new society

  1. We meet your association. We walk your parking with the facility manager and agree on timings, a water point and where the crew signs in.
  2. A free demo morning. We clean up to 10 residents’ cars for free, so people can judge the work before anyone pays.
  3. Residents sign up in a minute. A QR poster in each lift lobby opens a WhatsApp chat. No app and no paper forms.
  4. Daily service begins. The crew arrives at the agreed time every morning. Attendance and complaints go to your facility manager each month.

What it costs

Society rates start at 25 cars. For a society on the Daily plan, residents pay 15% less than the standard price per car. We send a proposal with per-car prices, crew size and timings after the walk-through. You can get a rough idea with the estimate on our homepage.

Do we need the association’s permission? For basement work, yes, and we handle it. We share crew documents, insurance details and timings with your facility manager before the first wash.
