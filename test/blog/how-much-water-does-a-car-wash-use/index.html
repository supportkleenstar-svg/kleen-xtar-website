2 litres or 150? How much water a car wash really uses in Bengaluru

Water24 November 20262 min read

A hose wash uses around 150 litres, a bucket wash 20 to 40. A rinseless daily clean uses about 2. Here’s what that means for one car and for a whole apartment society.

Every summer, Bengaluru apartment groups fill up with the same messages. Tanker prices are up, borewells are low and someone is washing their car with a hose in the basement again.

Washing a car doesn’t have to use much water at all. It depends entirely on the method.

Water per wash, by method

MethodWater per car
Hose washabout 150 litres
Bucket wash20 to 40 litres
Our doorstep foam washabout 20 litres
Our rinseless daily cleanabout 2 litres

How a car gets clean with 2 litres

A rinseless clean doesn’t rely on water to carry dirt away. The work is done by the shampoo and the cloth:

  1. Loose dust is lifted off with a dry microfibre duster.
  2. A rinseless shampoo goes on one panel at a time. It loosens the remaining dirt and wraps around it so it doesn’t scratch.
  3. A folded microfibre cloth picks the dirt up, a fresh side for every panel.

The two litres go into the wash solution and rinsing the cloth between panels, in two buckets with grit guards. There is no runoff, so there’s no puddle on your road and nothing running across the basement floor.

What it adds up to in a society

Take an apartment society where 60 cars are cleaned 26 times a month.

At 6,000 litres a tanker, that’s about 38 tanker loads a month that a society doesn’t have to buy. You can try your own numbers in the society estimate on our homepage.

Where our water comes from

Our crew carries water in sealed cans. In societies, we can use a tap the association approves instead, and we log what we use every day. No hose pipes, ever.

When does a car need more than 2 litres? After a road trip, the first rains or a lot of mud, a rinseless wipe isn’t enough. That’s what the doorstep foam wash is for: thick foam, a two-bucket mitt wash and a low-flow rinse, still about 20 litres in total.
