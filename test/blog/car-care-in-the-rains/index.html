Rain, mud and damp smells: looking after your car through Bengaluru’s rainy months

Seasonal24 November 20262 min read

What happens to your daily clean when it pours, why the first rains are the time for a proper wash, and how to stop the cabin smelling damp.

Bengaluru rain doesn’t clean a car. It drops the dust that was in the air onto your paint, splashes mud up the sides and leaves water spots when it dries. And a car that’s wet inside for a few days starts to smell.

What happens to your daily clean when it rains

  • Heavy rain: we wipe the glass and mirrors only, so you can see properly on the drive. The full clean moves to the end of your month, so you don’t lose it.
  • Light drizzle: the car is cleaned as usual.

Book a foam wash after the first rains

The first heavy rain of the season brings down a lot of dust and leaves a muddy film low on the doors and in the wheel arches. A daily wipe isn’t meant to shift that. A doorstep foam wash is: thick foam soaks for five minutes, a two-bucket mitt wash goes over every panel, and the door edges, fuel lid and wheel arches get cleaned too. It takes 45 to 60 minutes, uses about 20 litres of water and starts from ₹499.

Check your wipers before you need them

Wiper blades harden in the summer heat and only show it when the rain starts. If they leave streaks or judder across the glass, replace them. Our cleaners note worn wiper blades in their morning walk-around photos, so you’ll usually hear about it before the first storm.

Keep the cabin dry

  • Take wet umbrellas and raincoats out of the car rather than leaving them on the floor.
  • Lift the floor mats and let them dry if water has got under them.
  • Run the AC for a few minutes before you park, which helps clear moisture.
  • On a dry afternoon, leave the windows down for a while if the car is somewhere safe.

If the car already smells damp

Air fresheners only cover the smell. Our ozone odour treatment deals with it: the cabin is vacuumed and the source cleaned, an ozone generator runs in the closed car for 30 minutes, and the car is aired out for 15 minutes before anyone gets in. It takes about 45 minutes and starts from ₹699. If the seats or carpet are soaked, the interior deep clean shampoos and extracts the fabric and then leaves the car to dry with the windows down.
