About these terms

These terms apply when you book a free trial, a monthly plan or a one-time service with Kleen Xtar [registered business name and address, to confirm] in Bengaluru. By booking, you agree to them. We’ll tell you on WhatsApp before we change anything that affects your plan.

Our service

  • We clean your car where it’s parked: in your porch, on the street outside your gate, in your driveway or in your society’s parking.
  • Daily cleans happen between 4:45 and 7:15 AM, depending on your area. Your cleaner keeps roughly the same time every day.
  • After every clean, we send two photos on WhatsApp.
  • What each plan includes is listed on our plans section. Daily cleans run Monday to Saturday.

Free trial wash

Your first wash is free, one per household or car [to confirm]. There’s no obligation to buy a plan afterwards.

Plans, prices and payment

  • Prices are per car, per month, and depend on car type. They’re shown on our website at the time you book.
  • Plans run month to month. There’s no lock-in period and no deposit.
  • Paying every 3 months saves 10%.
  • For more than one car at the same home, the second car is 10% off and every car after that is 15% off.
  • You can pay by UPI autopay, card or net banking. You get a GST invoice every month on WhatsApp and email.
  • Society rates start at 25 cars and are agreed in a written proposal with your association.

Pausing, skipping and cancelling

  • Send “pause” on WhatsApp with your dates. Paused and skipped days move to the end of your billing month, up to 15 days a month.
  • You can cancel any time before your next billing date. Your plan runs to the end of the month you’ve paid for. [Refunds for unused days, to confirm.]

Rain days

On heavy rain days we wipe the glass and mirrors only, and the full clean moves to the end of your month. Light drizzle days are cleaned as usual.

If you’re not happy with a wash

Send a photo on WhatsApp before 10 AM. Your cleaner comes back the same day and cleans it again at no charge. On the Alternate days plan, a re-clean happens on the next visit; on Daily Plus, within 4 hours.

Keys and access

  • We only need your key on interior days. You can leave it with security, your housekeeper or in a lockbox.
  • Keys go into a sealed pouch and every handover is logged with the time.
  • You’re responsible for making sure our crew is allowed into your parking area. For societies, we arrange access with the association.

Your car

  • Before the first clean, our cleaner walks around the car and photographs existing marks, scratches and dents. We do the same whenever we notice something new.
  • Please remove valuables from the car before interior days. [Liability for items left in the car, to confirm.]
  • If you believe we caused damage, tell us on WhatsApp within [24 hours, to confirm] with photos. We’ll look into it and reply in writing. [Liability limits and insurance cover, to confirm.]
