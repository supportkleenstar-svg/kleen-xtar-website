Clean cars before Bengaluru wakes up

Why we do it this way

Most people in Bengaluru leave for work with a dusty car, or rely on a cleaner who turns up when they can, uses whatever is to hand and is gone before anyone can check. Some use kerosene on the tyres and a single rag for the whole car.

We wanted the opposite: a clean you can count on every morning, done properly, without wasting water and without anyone having to wake up for it.

So every daily clean follows the same seven steps. The cleaner walks around the car first and photographs anything new. Loose dust comes off before anything wet touches the paint. Glass, wheels and cabin each get their own cloth. And the morning ends with a photo on your phone, not a knock on your door.

Everything runs on WhatsApp: photos, reminders, pauses and invoices. There’s no app to install.

What you can count on

The same cleaner every morning

One person looks after your car, knows where it’s parked and notices when something changes.

A photo, every time

Two photos on WhatsApp after every clean, with the time and your cleaner’s name. You don’t have to go down to check.

Gentle on paint

Loose dust first, a rinseless shampoo, straight-line wiping and a fresh set of cloths for every car.

Water is precious here

About 2 litres a car for a daily clean, carried in by our crew. No hose pipes and no runoff.

A crew you can trust

Every cleaner is police-verified, trained for two weeks alongside a senior crew lead, and works in uniform with photo ID.

No lock-in

Plans run month to month. Pause by WhatsApp when you travel, cancel any time before your next billing date.
