What working with us is like

Your day starts early and ends early

Daily cleans happen between 4:45 and 7:15 AM, so most of the day is your own.

Two weeks of paid training

You work alongside a senior crew lead until you’re confident on every step.

Uniform and photo ID

You’ll look professional and customers and security will know who you are.

Proper kit and products

Colour-coded microfibre, two buckets, a cordless vacuum and safe products. No kerosene, ever.

Your work gets seen

Customers see your photos every morning and many rate the wash on WhatsApp.

Steady routes

The same cars, at the same time, close to where you work.
