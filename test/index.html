What happens to your car before you wake up

Every daily clean follows the same seven steps, in the same order. Scroll to follow one morning with Ravi, your cleaner, from 5:40 to 6:15 AM.

Step 7 of 7: Final check and photo

  1. 05:40

    Check in and walk around

    Ravi signs in at the gate at 4:45 and reaches your car at its usual time. He switches on a headlamp and walks around the car once before touching it. Anything new, like a scratch or a soft tyre, gets a photo and a note on WhatsApp.

    • Photo ID and uniform
    • New marks photographed first
    • About 2 minutes
  2. 05:42

    Lift the loose dust

    Bengaluru dust is fine and gritty. Wiping it straight away is what causes swirl marks, so loose dust comes off first with a soft duster, roof to bumper, without pressing down.

    • Soft microfibre duster
    • No pressure on the paint
    • About 3 minutes
  3. 05:45

    Foam on, top to bottom

    A pH-neutral rinseless shampoo goes on one panel at a time, starting at the roof. It loosens the film of dirt that’s left and coats the paint, so the cloth glides instead of scraping.

    • pH-neutral, wax-safe shampoo
    • Mixed fresh each morning
    • About 4 minutes
  4. 05:49

    Wipe, one panel at a time

    A folded microfibre cloth wipes each panel in straight lines, never circles. Ravi turns to a fresh side after every panel, so dirt from one panel never touches the next.

    • Blue cloth for paint only
    • 8 clean sides per cloth
    • About 7 minutes
  5. 05:56

    Glass, wheels and tyres

    Glass and mirrors get an ammonia-free cleaner and a waffle-weave cloth for a streak-free finish. Wheels get their own brush and cloth, then a water-based dressing on the tyres.

    • Lilac cloth for glass
    • Charcoal cloth for wheels
    • Safe on tinted film
  6. 06:02

    Interior, on interior days

    Once or twice a week, depending on your plan. Mats come out to be beaten and vacuumed, then the seats, floor and boot. Dashboard, steering wheel, AC vents and door pads are wiped with a cleaner that protects them from the sun.

    • Cordless vacuum, crevice tool
    • Marigold cloth for the cabin
    • About 10 minutes
  7. 06:12

    Final check and photo

    One more walk around, now in daylight, then two photos to your WhatsApp. Ravi picks up any litter, checks the parking spot is dry and moves on to the next car.

    • Photo sent before 7:15 AM
    • Spot left dry and clean
    • Re-clean promise until 10 AM

That’s about 35 minutes on an interior day and 25 on the others. You were asleep for all of it. See what Ravi carries

One-time services at your doorstep

For a deeper clean, a fix or a one-off. Book them on their own or add them to a plan. Prices shown are for a hatchback.

Doorstep foam wash

Time
45 to 60 minutes
Price
From ₹499
Good for
After a road trip or the first rains

What happens

  1. Thick foam goes on and soaks for five minutes to loosen road grime.
  2. A soft mitt washes every panel using two buckets, one for soap and one for rinsing.
  3. A low-flow rinse takes off the foam. The whole wash uses about 20 litres.
  4. Door edges, fuel lid and wheel arches get cleaned too.
  5. Mirrors and grille are air-blown and the car is hand-dried with a drying towel.

Interior deep clean

Time
2.5 to 3 hours
Price
From ₹1,499
Good for
Spills, pet hair, musty smells, or before a sale

What happens

  1. Mats, seats and carpets are vacuumed, with crevice tools for the tight gaps.
  2. Fabric seats are shampooed and extracted. Leather is cleaned and conditioned.
  3. The roof lining is spot-cleaned and AC vents are brushed out.
  4. Door pads, dashboard and console are cleaned and protected.
  5. An odour neutraliser goes in, then the car dries for two hours with the windows down.

Wax and polish

Time
About 3 hours
Price
From ₹1,999
Good for
Dull paint and light swirl marks

What happens

  1. A full wash, then a clay bar lifts dirt that’s stuck to the paint.
  2. A machine polish reduces light swirls and brings back the gloss.
  3. A synthetic sealant or carnauba wax goes on and is buffed off.
  4. Faded black plastic trims are restored.

How we start in a new society

  1. We meet your association

    A QR poster in each lift lobby opens a WhatsApp chat. No app and no paper forms.

    The crew arrives at the agreed time every morning. Attendance and complaints go to your facility manager each month.
